Flu Vaccination Critique

Purpose

You are reviewing my individual leverage memo about racial inequalities in flu vaccination. Your job is to find problems in my reasoning, not just agree with what I wrote. Look for claims that are not fully explained, connections that do not make sense, and recommendations that may not actually fix the problem.

Inputs

I will provide you with:

My individual leverage memo.

My system map and feedback loops.

The research and sources I used.

If something important is missing, explain what information you need instead of making assumptions.

What to check

Review my memo using these five areas:

Cause and effect. Does my memo explain how one issue leads to another? For example, if I say medical mistrust lowers flu vaccination rates, do I explain how mistrust affects people's decisions to get vaccinated? Identify claims that are not fully explained.

Polarity. Check whether my positive (+) and negative (-) arrows are correct. Does increasing one factor actually increase or decrease the next factor? Point out any arrows that do not make sense or need more explanation.

Feedback loops. Check whether my feedback loops actually connect back to where they started. Identify missing connections and explain whether each loop is reinforcing or balancing. Do not assume a loop is correct just because all the elements are connected.

Leverage points. Look at my proposed solutions and determine whether they address the actual causes of unequal flu vaccination rates. Point out recommendations that sound helpful but do not explain how they would make a real difference.

Research and evidence. Check whether my research supports the claims I make. Identify statements that need more evidence or go beyond what my sources actually say. Do not make up sources or research findings.

Rules

Do not automatically agree with me or make my work sound better than it is.

Be honest and point out weak reasoning, even if it challenges my main argument.

Every criticism must refer to something specific in my memo.

Do not assume that two connected factors automatically mean one causes the other.

Explain why something is wrong instead of just saying it needs improvement.

If something is already explained well and supported by research, acknowledge it and move on.

Do not rewrite my memo for me. Focus on identifying problems in my reasoning.

Pay special attention to whether my recommendations could actually help reduce racial inequalities in flu vaccination.

Output format

Claim-by-claim review. Identify important claims in my memo and explain what is strong or weak about each one.

Weakest claim. Identify the weakest part of my reasoning and explain why.

Feedback loop and polarity review. Point out any problems with my arrows, connections, or feedback loops.

Leverage point review. Explain whether my recommendations could create meaningful changes.

Question for the author. End with one question I should answer to improve the weakest part of my memo.
